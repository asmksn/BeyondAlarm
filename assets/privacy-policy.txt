Dynamic Alarm — Privacy Policy
Effective date: 5 October 2026

Dynamic Alarm is provided by Kibria. Questions and privacy requests: asmksn@gmail.com.

Information on your phone
Alarm times, titles, quotes, selected destinations, message drafts, recipient details, and references to files you select are stored locally in the app. The app does not require an account and includes no advertising or analytics SDKs. It does not operate a developer-owned server. It does not access your GPS location. Android's pickers let you choose individual documents, contacts and ringtones. Installed launchable apps are listed locally only when you choose an app action.

Optional online features
Searching the map sends your entered search text to Photon (photon.komoot.io). Viewing a map requests tiles from OpenStreetMap (tile.openstreetmap.org); those requests identify the viewed map area. Requesting weather sends your entered city to Open-Meteo's geocoding service and the selected city's coordinates to its forecast service. These services receive your IP address and normal network request information and may keep server logs under their own policies. Requests use HTTPS. A searched or viewed place is not necessarily your current location. Map search results are cached locally for up to seven days and may remain stored until replaced or app data is cleared. Online features require an internet connection.

External actions and attachments
Calls, messages, emails, navigation, web pages and document actions open apps you choose. Information needed for the action, including recipient details, drafted messages, selected files or destinations, is passed to that app when you request the action. Dynamic Alarm does not automatically send emails or messages. Confirm sending in the external app. Its provider's privacy practices then apply. Email attachments are accessed through permissions granted by Android's document picker.

Alarm permissions
Notifications, exact alarms, full-screen alarm access, vibration and a foreground audio service support alarms you set. Reboot access restores schedules. These permissions do not upload alarm information. Titles and quotes may appear on the lock screen; control notification visibility in Android settings.

Retention and deletion
Delete an alarm from the home screen to remove its saved settings. Clearing Dynamic Alarm's app storage or uninstalling removes its local app data, including saved alarms and cached searches. Original selected files and copies already sent through another app are not deleted. If you email support, your email address, message and attachments reach the support mailbox and may be retained to handle your request; email asmksn@gmail.com to request deletion of support correspondence. We cannot delete data held by independent services; contact those services directly.
